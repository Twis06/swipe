    Set up the preview

    Use the MacBook’s built-in microphone. Keep the laptop on the same surface as your recordings.

    One swipe at a time

    1. Keep still for one second after starting.
    2. Up is toward the screen. Down is toward the palm rest.
    3. Lift your finger and wait for the result before the next swipe.

    Allow about a second for each result. An uncertain result means the sound did not match clearly enough.

    What this preview does

    Audio is processed by the server on this Mac. Preview audio and results are not saved to disk, and no shortcuts or system actions run.
