How this data is saved

One raw float WAV and its prompt timeline are saved locally. Prompt times describe what was shown, not verified gesture timing. Free movement is saved as an unlabeled up/down sequence for later review.

The microphone disconnects when recording finishes or you cancel. Hiding this tab cancels an unfinished take. A failed save can be retried while the finished audio remains in memory.

If this folder is inside a cloud-synced directory, your sync provider may upload saved recordings.
